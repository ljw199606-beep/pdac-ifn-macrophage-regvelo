# Third-Party Data Provenance, Download Dates, and Redistribution Terms

All primary third-party datasets analyzed in this study remain hosted at their official repositories and are **not** redistributed in raw form (FASTQ, BAM, `.h5ad`, or Visium archives). Our Zenodo archive redistributes exclusively **original analysis code (MIT License), provenance manifests, and derived aggregate statistical tables / per-panel Figure Source Data (CC BY 4.0)** containing zero controlled-access human sequencing reads, zero germline genotypes, zero individually identifiable patient records, and zero credentials or tokens.
